# NOVEMBER LOCK IN

**30 Days. Five Pillars. One Commitment.**

November Lock In is a personal development and habit-tracking project designed around a 30-day self-improvement challenge.

The goal is simple: build consistency, strengthen discipline, and make measurable progress through small daily actions.

This project is built around one principle:

> «Hardcore doesn't mean exhausting yourself. It means keeping the promises you make to yourself.»

---

## Five Pillars of Development

The challenge focuses on five essential areas of personal growth.

| Pillar | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Glow Up | Skincare, grooming, appearance and personal style |
| Fitness | Strength, physique, mobility and endurance |
| Japanese | Grammar, vocabulary, listening and speaking |
| Lifestyle | Sleep, nutrition, organization and daily routines |
| Reading | Knowledge, critical thinking and intellectual growth |

> The former **Mental** pillar (journaling and reflection) is no longer part of daily tracking or scoring. It is hidden in the app; anything saved earlier stays in storage and in backups.

## The 30-Day Challenge

The challenge runs from November 1 to November 30, 2026.

It is divided into four development phases and a final review.

- Week 01 — Foundation: Establish consistent daily routines.
- Week 02 — Discipline: Build consistency and eliminate unnecessary distractions.
- Week 03 — Intensity: Challenge your current limits and improve performance.
- Week 04 — Transformation: Strengthen your habits and evaluate your progress.
- Final Days — Review: Compare your starting point with your results.

## Daily Tracking System

The challenge uses a five-point daily scoring system.

Each pillar contributes one point toward the daily score. One tap marks a pillar as done. Sets, reps, minutes and pages are optional extras, and a reduced version of a planned session still counts.

- Glow Up — 1 point
- Fitness — 1 point
- Japanese — 1 point
- Lifestyle — 1 point
- Reading — 1 point

**Maximum daily score:** 5 points

**Maximum 30-day score:** 150 points

An optional Deep Focus timer (25, 50 or 60 minutes) is available in the app. It does not affect the score.

## Adaptive Habit System

Every pillar has three optional completion levels: **Minimum** (1 pt), **Standard** (2 pts) and **High** (3 pts). Picking any level marks the pillar done, so a Minimum day still counts toward 5/5. Tap the selected level again to undo. The 1–2–3 points are shown separately on the Progress tab and are never added to the 150-point score. A pillar ticked without a level counts as Standard.

## Performance Levels

| Total Score | Share of 150 | Performance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 135–150 | 90% and above | Excellent |
| 120–134 | 80–89% | Strong |
| 98–119 | 65–79% | Moderate |
| Below 98 | Below 65% | Keep going |

Missing a day does not mean failure. The objective is to maintain consistency and return to the system after setbacks.

## Progress Evaluation

Three checkpoints define the challenge:

1. **November 1 — Baseline:** Record your starting condition, fitness performance and Japanese speaking ability.
2. **November 15 — Midpoint:** Evaluate your progress and identify areas for improvement.
3. **November 30 — Final Review:** Compare your results and measure your development.

## Core Philosophy

This is not about becoming perfect in 30 days.

It is about becoming more disciplined, capable and intentional with your time.

- Consistency over motivation.
- Progress over perfection.
- Discipline over temporary excitement.
- Recovery over burnout.
- Proof over promises.
